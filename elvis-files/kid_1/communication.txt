**Networking & Internet Layers**
### **Application Layer**

- Provides **user interface**
- Handles **encryption, authentication, security**
- Manages application-level communication

---

### **Transport Layer**

- Ensures **end-to-end communication**
- Splits data into **segments**
- Provides:
    - Error control
    - Flow control

---

### **Internet Layer**

- Identifies **sender and receiver (IP address)**
- Adds headers to packets
- Routes packets through network

---

### **Link Layer (Data Link Layer)**

- Handles **physical transmission**
- Converts datagrams into **frames**
- Maps **IP address → MAC address**
